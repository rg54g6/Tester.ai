About the test

The assessment evaluates the ability to support customers, communicate clearly, reason through ambiguous situations, and use AI responsibly in daily work.

The vital skills assessed include:

  • Handling difficult customer situations with empathy and structure.
  • Identifying underlying issues and choosing appropriate resolutions.
  • Logical, numerical, and verbal reasoning for business and customer needs.
  • Knowing when AI can help and when human judgement is required.
